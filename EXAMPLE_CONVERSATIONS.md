# Example Conversations - Hybrid Chatbot with Clarifying Questions

This shows what practitioners will experience when using the updated chatbot.

## Example 1: Funding a Tutoring Position

### User Asks:
```
Can we use Subpart 2 funds to pay for a reading tutor?
```

### Bot Responds (Hybrid Approach):

**✅ Quick Answer:**
Yes, supplemental reading tutoring is an allowable use of Title I, Part D, Subpart 2 funds under Section 12 (Academic Services).

**❓ To give you the most accurate guidance for YOUR situation, I need to understand:**

- **Question 1:** Will this tutor work primarily with students returning from correctional facilities, or with at-risk students in school settings, or both?

- **Question 2:** Is this tutoring completely supplemental (beyond the core reading instruction all students receive), or will it partially replace services already provided by the district?

- **Question 3:** How will you document that participating students have an identified reading deficit and track that the tutoring is providing measurable improvement?

**Once you answer these questions, I can provide:**
- Specific compliance requirements for your situation
- Time and effort documentation requirements
- How to ensure it meets the supplemental standard
- Cost allocation if shared with other populations

## Example 2: At-Risk Student Eligibility

### User Asks:
```
Is a student who's failing two classes considered at-risk?
```

### Bot Responds:

**✅ Quick Answer:**
Not necessarily. Simply failing classes doesn't automatically qualify someone as at-risk under Title I, Part D, Subpart 2. The federal definition requires systemic risk factors (Section 13).

**❓ To make sure your eligibility determination is compliant, I need to know:**

- **Question 1:** What is causing the student's failure? (e.g., Are they involved with juvenile justice, substance abuse, chronically absent, over-age for their grade, previously dropped out?)

- **Question 2:** Is the student exhibiting multiple risk factors, or is this just academic struggle in isolation?

- **Question 3:** Does your LEA have a formal eligibility determination process documented? Have you considered whether the student should receive Title I, Part A services instead?

**Once you provide context, I can:**
- Confirm whether they meet the statutory at-risk definition
- Explain the difference between general academic struggle and Subpart 2-eligible at-risk
- Help you document the eligibility determination
- Recommend the right program for this student

## Example 3: Cost Allocation for Shared Staffing

### User Asks:
```
Can we use Subpart 2 funds to pay half of a coordinator's salary?
```

### Bot Responds:

**✅ Quick Answer:**
Yes, you can charge a proportional share of salary costs if the position serves eligible students and you document the allocation with time and effort tracking (Section 16).

**❓ To ensure your cost allocation is compliant, I need clarity on:**

- **Question 1:** What is this coordinator's primary role? (e.g., transition coordination, academic services coordination, or general school counseling?)

- **Question 2:** How much time will they actually spend on direct services to eligible students vs. administrative/coordination duties? (This affects the 75% direct services requirement)

- **Question 3:** Will you use sign-in sheets, time logs, or another method to document the actual hours spent on Subpart 2 activities?

**Once I understand these details, I can explain:**
- Whether this position counts toward the 75% direct services minimum or the 20% coordination maximum
- Proper time and effort tracking requirements
- Allocation methodology to document
- Monitoring documentation you'll need

## Example 4: Summer Programming Decision

### User Asks:
```
Can we fund a general summer school program?
```

### Bot Responds:

**✅ Quick Answer:**
Not without additional conditions. General summer school available to all students cannot be funded with Subpart 2 dollars unless you provide supplemental services specifically for eligible youth (Section 12).

**❓ To determine if your situation qualifies, clarify:**

- **Question 1:** Are you planning to provide supplemental academic services exclusively to Subpart 2-eligible students during the summer program, or is this just general summer school for everyone?

- **Question 2:** If supplemental, what specific services? (e.g., targeted credit recovery, literacy intervention, CTE pathway acceleration?)

- **Question 3:** Can you identify which students are eligible and track that they're receiving more than general summer school offers?

**Once you answer, I can tell you:**
- If supplemental summer services are allowable
- How to structure the program to be compliant
- Documentation requirements
- Cost allocation if mixed populations attend

## Example 5: Program Design and Needs Assessment

### User Asks:
```
What should we include in our program design?
```

### Bot Responds:

**✅ Quick Answer:**
Your program design should be built on your Comprehensive Needs Assessment (CNA) and include academic, transition, and career components tailored to identified student needs (Section 10).

**❓ Before diving into components, help me understand your context:**

- **Question 1:** Does your LEA primarily serve delinquent youth returning from facilities, at-risk youth in schools, or both?

- **Question 2:** What are your top 3 identified needs for eligible youth? (e.g., credit deficiency, attendance, limited CTE pathways, lack of transition support?)

- **Question 3:** Have you completed a Comprehensive Needs Assessment in the last 3 years, and do you have data showing which needs are highest priority?

**Once I know this, I can:**
- Recommend which program components fit your specific student population
- Help you align funding to your top needs
- Explain what's required in your application narrative
- Ensure your program is data-driven

## How Practitioners Will Interact

1. **They ask a question** - No need to provide context upfront
2. **Bot gives quick answer** - They know the basic answer immediately
3. **Bot asks clarifying questions** - They provide their specific situation
4. **Bot gives detailed guidance** - Completely tailored to their context
5. **They have the answer they need** - With all compliance requirements

The conversation feels natural and conversational, not like a form to fill out.

## Real-World Example: Full Conversation

```
PRACTITIONER: "Can we use funds to pay a teacher who provides credit recovery?"

BOT: "Yes, credit recovery instruction is an allowable use of funds under Section 12, 
provided it's supplemental.

To give you specific compliance guidance:
- Question 1: Is this teacher working only with Subpart 2-eligible students, or will 
  some non-eligible students also participate in the credit recovery?
- Question 2: Is the LEA otherwise required to provide credit recovery to all students, 
  or is this a new supplemental service for eligible youth?
- Question 3: How will you document which students participated and verify they earned 
  required credits?

Please answer these so I can confirm this is compliant and explain your documentation 
requirements."

PRACTITIONER: "The teacher works with 8 students returning from the facility and 2 
at-risk students from our school. The district doesn't normally offer credit recovery, 
so it's all supplemental. We'll track participation by sign-in sheets and credit attainment 
by transcript review."

BOT: "Perfect. Here's your compliance status:

✅ ALLOWABLE: Your credit recovery teacher is fully compliant because:
- Serving only eligible students (facility returns + identified at-risk)
- Completely supplemental (district doesn't otherwise provide it)
- Strong documentation method (sign-in + transcripts)

SECTION 12 REQUIREMENTS YOU'RE MEETING:
- Supplemental nature: ✅ (not replacing baseline services)
- Identified need: ✅ (students are credit-deficient)
- Direct service: ✅ (counts toward 75% minimum)
- Documented outcomes: ✅ (tracking credit attainment)

DOCUMENTATION YOU SHOULD MAINTAIN:
- Sign-in sheets showing student participation
- Credit accrual reports (before/after)
- Lesson plans tied to state standards
- Time logs showing hours of instruction
- CNA data justifying this service

COST ALLOCATION:
- 100% of salary can be charged to Subpart 2 (serves only eligible students)

This is an excellent use of funds. You're meeting all requirements."
```
